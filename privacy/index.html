Privacy Policy

Effective 14 October 2026

This explains what personal information Adextry collects, why, and what your rights are under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023.

What we collect

  • Account details: your email address and password (stored securely, never in plain text).
  • Business details: your name, business name, category, phone number, address and website.
  • Ads you upload: images and videos, and the campaigns you create.
  • Payment details: payment references and amounts. Card details are handled by Paystack and never reach us. Screen owners also give bank details to Paystack, and we keep only the account name, bank and last four digits.
  • Screen activity: player status, play logs and the times a screen last checked in.
  • Technical data: IP address, browser type and security logs, and the cookies needed to keep you signed in.

How we use it

  • To run your account, review and play ads, and take and send payments.
  • To keep Adextry secure and to prevent fraud.
  • To email you about your account, campaigns, payments and screens.
  • To meet legal and accounting duties.

Who sees it

  • Screen owners see an advertiser's business name and the ad itself, so they can approve it.
  • Paystack processes payments. Our email, hosting and file-storage providers handle data for us under their own security commitments.
  • We share information with authorities only when the law requires it. We do not sell your personal information.

How long we keep it

  • Rejected or failed ads are deleted 30 days after they were last touched.
  • Account information is kept while your account is open.
  • Payment and tax records are kept for as long as the law requires, even after an account is deleted.

Your rights

You can ask to see your information, correct it, object to how we use it, or have it deleted. To request deletion, use the "Request account deletion" button on your Settings page, or email hello@adextry.io. We will reply within 30 days. If you are unhappy with our answer, you can complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.

Security

We use encryption in transit, hashed passwords, signed payment confirmations and access controls. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique password.

Children

Adextry is not for anyone under 18.

Changes and contact

We will update this page when our practices change. Questions: hello@adextry.io.
