How Adextry works

One platform, two journeys — one network.

Whether you're placing a campaign or hosting a screen, Adextry handles the entire lifecycle automatically.

01

Choose your screens and dates

Browse screens by location and price, then pick your slots per day and how long to run. The full price is shown before you pay anything.

02

Upload your ad

Add an image or a video (JPG, PNG, WebP, MP4, MOV or WebM). We check the format and size for you.

03

Get approved

Adextry checks your content, then the screen owner approves it for their screen. Both checks happen before you pay.

04

Pay securely

Pay in naira with Paystack. Payments are final once you pay, so check your screens and dates first.

05

Go live and track every play

Your ad joins the screen's rotation. Every airing is recorded as proof-of-play, and a missed day is made up for free on Adextry player screens.

01

Register and get verified

Create your account and add your business details. We verify every screen owner before they can list screens.

02

List your screen

Add the location, your price per slot, operating hours and slots per loop. Advertisers can then find and book it.

03

Connect your player

Install the Adextry Player on an Android box and enter the pairing code in your dashboard. Already use a CMS? Connect it instead. Player setup guide.

04

Approve campaigns

Review each request and approve what you want on your screen. Approved ads join your rotation automatically.

05

Get paid automatically

Your share of every payment goes straight to your bank account through Paystack. There is nothing to withdraw.

End-to-End Architecture

How the network connects.

01

Advertiser Portal

Uploads media & sets target schedules

02

Adextry Platform

Approves, schedules & routes payloads

03

Android Player or your CMS

Downloads media & plays on schedule, even offline

04

Physical Screen

Displays live ad to target audience

Play logs sync back from the player or your CMS to Adextry, so advertisers and screen owners see proof-of-play in their dashboards.
