Questions

Good to know before you start.

How much does it cost?
Each screen owner sets a price per slot. You see the full price for your screens, slots and dates before you pay, and you pay in naira.
Who approves my ad?
Two checks: Adextry reviews the content first, then the screen owner approves it for their screen. You only pay after both are done.
Can I cancel or get a refund?
You can cancel for free until your campaign is approved. After you pay, bookings are final and not refundable. If your ad does not play on a booked day because of a problem on our side or the screen's, we add a make-up day at no cost. See the terms for details.
How do I know my ad played?
Every airing is logged as proof-of-play, with the time, and you can see the log in your dashboard.
What files can I upload?
Images (JPG, PNG, WebP) up to 10 MB and videos (MP4, MOV, WebM) up to 500 MB. We check the format and size for you.
What do I need to host a screen?
A screen, an Android TV box or stick (Android 8.0 or newer), and an internet connection. Install the free Adextry Player and pair it with the code on the screen. Read the setup guide.
How do screen owners get paid?
When an advertiser pays, your share is sent straight to your bank account through Paystack. There is nothing to withdraw.
