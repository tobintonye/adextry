About Adextry

We're building the operating layer for physical screens.

Out-of-home advertising has always been analog at heart bookings by email, spreadsheets for scheduling, and no real proof an ad ever played. Adextry turns every screen into a connected, accountable node in a single network.

Our mission

To make buying and running digital out-of-home advertising as simple, transparent, and measurable as buying an ad online for advertisers of any size, and screen owners of any scale.

Our vision

A world where any physical screen a storefront display, a roadside billboard, a mall panel can be discovered, booked, and verified in minutes, not weeks.

The problem we saw

The tools to run digital out-of-home well already exist automated scheduling, remote content delivery, real-time monitoring have been solved for years. But they're built for large networks and big media budgets, sold through agencies and enterprise contracts. Independent screen owners and smaller advertisers are mostly locked out of that infrastructure left to manage bookings manually, over email and spreadsheets, with no easy way for an advertiser to confirm a campaign actually ran, where, or how often.

Adextry brings that same operational clarity to the segment the bigger platforms don't serve: a self-serve marketplace where an advertiser can pick a screen, book a slot, and pay no agency, no salesperson, no negotiation while screen owners list their inventory and get paid automatically, without needing enterprise software or a media buying relationship to participate.

Our Android players check in on their own pulling each day's schedule automatically and reporting their status back so screen owners don't need a technician on-site just to keep things running. Every airing is logged the moment it plays, tied to the exact slot and campaign it fulfilled giving advertisers real proof their ad ran, and giving screen owners a verifiable record of what's aired on their screen and when.

Every campaign passes through a two-step review before it airs first for content and platform safety, then a final check from the screen owner it's booked on so nothing reaches a live billboard without a human check on both sides. And every payment is split and routed automatically the moment a campaign is confirmed, with a full audit trail behind it, so screen owners always know exactly what they're owed and why.
