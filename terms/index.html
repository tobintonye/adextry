These terms are an agreement between you and Adextry ("we", "us"). By creating an account you agree to them and to our Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use Adextry.

1. What Adextry is

Adextry is a marketplace. Advertisers book time on digital screens, and screen owners earn money by showing those ads. We provide the platform, the review process and the player software. We do not own the screens listed by screen owners.

2. Your account

  • You must be 18 or older, or the legal representative of a registered business.
  • Give accurate information and keep it up to date. We may ask you to verify your identity or business before you can book or list screens.
  • Keep your password private. You are responsible for what happens under your account.

3. Advertisers

  • You must own, or have permission to use, everything in your ad.
  • Ads must not be illegal, misleading, hateful, sexually explicit, or harmful, and must follow Nigerian advertising rules.
  • Every ad is reviewed by us and by the screen owner. Either may reject it. Approval is not a guarantee that an ad will run, and an approval expires if you do not pay in time.
  • Ads that are rejected, or that fail our checks, are deleted from our systems 30 days after they were last touched.

4. Screen owners

  • You must have the right to show ads on every screen you list, including any permits, landlord consent or CMS licences that apply.
  • Describe your screens honestly and keep your Adextry player powered and online. If you connect your own screen system, you must keep the places you reserved for Adextry free.
  • We may suspend an account that lists screens it cannot deliver, ignores campaign requests, or breaks these terms.

5. Prices and payments

  • The price, including our platform fee, is shown before you pay. Payments are processed by Paystack. We never see or store your card details.
  • When an advertiser pays, Paystack sends the screen owner's share to the owner's bank account and the platform fee to us.
  • An unpaid booking is released if payment is not completed within 24 hours.

6. Cancellations and refunds

  • You can cancel a campaign for free until it is approved. If you do not pay after approval, the approval expires and you are not charged.
  • Payments are final. Once a campaign is approved and paid for, it cannot be cancelled and the payment is not refundable.
  • If your paid ad does not play on a day it was booked because of a problem on our side or the screen's side (for example the screen was offline), we add a make-up day on the same screen at no extra cost, and email you the new date. We look for a free day for up to 60 days after your campaign's original end date. If there is none, we will contact you to agree new dates.
  • Rescheduling does not apply where the problem was caused by you, such as content that breaks our rules or details you gave us that were wrong.
  • If you dispute a payment with your bank, we may suspend your account while it is resolved.

7. What we are not responsible for

Screens depend on power, internet and third-party systems, so we cannot promise that an ad will play without interruption. To the extent the law allows, our responsibility to you for any claim is limited to the amount you paid us for the campaign involved, and we are not liable for indirect losses such as lost sales.

8. Ending your account

You can ask us to delete your account at any time from your Settings page. We may suspend or close accounts that break these terms. Paid campaigns will be run to completion before an account is closed.
