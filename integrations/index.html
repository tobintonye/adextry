How it works

Connect once. Adextry does the rest.

Step 1

Connect your CMS

In your dashboard, open the screen, choose your CMS and enter what we ask for: an API token, the container where Adextry ads should go, and how many places in your loop you have reserved for Adextry.

  • Your API token is stored encrypted and never shown again
  • Adextry never sells more places than you reserved
Step 2

Approved ads arrive on their own

When an advertiser pays for a campaign on your screen, Adextry creates the content and the schedule in your CMS for the booked days and hours. You upload nothing.

  • Only campaigns you approved are delivered
  • If delivery fails, our team is alerted and fixes it or reschedules the campaign
Step 3

Plays come back as proof

Your CMS reports each play to Adextry, so advertisers get proof-of-play. You set this up once in your CMS with a key we give you.

  • One-time setup in your CMS
  • Advertisers and owners see the same play log

Questions

About CMS integrations.

Do I still need the Android player?
No. A screen is run by either the Adextry player or your CMS. If your CMS already drives the screen, connect the CMS and skip the player.
Is my API token safe?
Yes. We store it encrypted and never display it again after you save it. You can replace it at any time.
Will Adextry fill my whole loop?
No. You choose how many places to reserve for Adextry, and we never sell more than that, so booked ads always fit.
When can I use it?
CMS integrations are in preview with a small group of screen owners. Contact us and we will let you know when yours is ready.
